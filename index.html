About

I am an Associate professor of applied mathematics at IRIT (UMR CNRS 5505), ENSEEIHT (Dépt. SN). My research focuses on optimal control, with a particular interest in geometric methods, numerical algorithms, and applications across engineering and the sciences.

Affiliations

  • IRIT — UMR CNRS 5505
  • ENSEEIHT — Département SN
  • Team — Algo. Parallèles et Optimisation

Research Interests

  • Optimal control theory and applications
  • Differential geometry and geometric control
  • Numerical algorithms for optimal control
  • Time-optimal problems, bang-bang controls, singular arcs
  • Applications in various fields
